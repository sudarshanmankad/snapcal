Fitness Move (when unlocked)

SnapCal can’t call HealthKit itself (needs Apple’s $99 native app). Shortcuts can read today’s Active Energy while the phone is unlocked, copy it, then SnapCal picks it up.

Auto-copy is not inside SnapCal — it’s in the Shortcuts app Automation tab.

1) Keep your existing shortcut that ends with Copy to Clipboard (rename it SnapCal Move if you want).
2) Shortcuts app → bottom Automation → + → Time of Day.
3) Pick a clock time (example 12:00) → Daily → Next.
4) Add action Run Shortcut → choose SnapCal Move (or your copy shortcut) → Done.
5) Open that automation → turn Ask Before Running OFF → confirm Don’t Ask.

Apple has no “every hour” button — make another Time of Day automation for each time you want (e.g. 8:00, 12:00, 16:00, 20:00). Phone must be unlocked for Health to copy.

Paste Move opens a box: long-press → Paste once (no second Paste). Or enable auto-read on open after your Shortcut copies Move.

“Clear today” removes only today’s meals/Move — not other days, not your food dictionary, not your Gemini key.
